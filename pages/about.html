About us

Introductory paragraph about the people, place and purpose.

How it started

The land was passed down through generations with more than 40 acres. The vast land is filled with fruits and ponds, mixed with various wild animals. The farm is family-based and managed by the family members with generations of dedication, hard work and agricultural tradition.

Through continous effort, teamwork and commitment to responsible farming practices, Kaznisah Farming aims to provide quality livestock while prioritising animal welfare and sustainable farm management

Practices and values

Sustainability

We aim to promote sustainable farming by using available resources responsibly, reducing unnecessary waste and maintaining the farm's natural environment for future generations.

Animal welfare

We recognise the importance of animal welfare by prioritising proper feeding, clean living conditions and attentive care to support the health and well-being of our livestock.

Land stewardship

Our family values the land passed down through generations. We aim to preserve its natural surrroundings through resposible land management and careful agricultural practices.

Certifications

We recognise the importance of meeting relevant agricultural standards and maintaining resposible farming practices as our business continues to develop.
