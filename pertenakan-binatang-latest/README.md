**Raised the old-fashioned way.**

## What you can do on the website

| If you are... | You can... |
|---|---|
| A visitor (no account) | Read about the farm, browse and filter the animals, see photos and details, and send the farm a message |
| A buyer (free account) | Everything above, plus save animals you like, send an inquiry to buy, and read and answer the farm's replies |
| Farm staff | Manage the herd records, add animals and photos, and answer inquiries and messages (staff accounts are created by the farm owner) |

## How to use it

### Browse the animals
1. Open **Our Animals** in the menu, or choose a type: Cattle, Goats, Sheep, Bulls, Poultry or Horses.
2. Use the filters (breed, age, sex, price, availability) and the sort menu to narrow the list.
3. Select **View details** on an animal to see its photos, age, weight, lineage and health summary.

### Ask about an animal
There is **no online payment**. You send an inquiry and the owners contact you to arrange everything.
1. Open an animal and choose **Send an Inquiry to Buy**.
2. Log in, or create a free buyer account (name, email and password; phone is optional).
3. Write a short message and send it. You will get a reference number.
4. Read the farm's reply under **Messages** in your account. You can answer there too.
5. An inquiry is **not a reservation**. The farm confirms availability and price with you directly.

### Save animals
Select **Save** on an animal card (you need to be logged in). Your saved animals are listed on your account page.

## Questions

**Do I pay on the website?** No. The website never takes payment. The farm arranges viewing, price and collection with you.

**Is every animal on the website still available?** Each animal shows a status: Available, Reserved or Sold. Contact the farm to confirm before travelling.

**Do I need an account?** Only to save animals, send an inquiry to buy, or read replies. Browsing and sending a contact message do not need one.

**I forgot my password.** On the login page, type your email and choose **Forgot password?**. A reset link is emailed to you.

## Your information

When you create an account or send a message, the website stores your name, email address, phone number (if you give one) and your messages. The farm's staff can read your inquiries and messages so they can reply. Your saved animals are kept on your account. No payment details are collected. Please do not put passwords or bank details in a message.
